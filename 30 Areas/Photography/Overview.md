# Photography

Search tags: #area/photography #photography #workflow

## Responsibility

Maintain a repeatable photography workflow covering planning, capture, selection, editing, delivery, and reusable creative references.

## Operating standards

- Record the shoot purpose, subject, location, timing, required deliverables, and constraints before capture.
- Keep capture notes, editing decisions, client requirements, and final delivery status distinguishable.
- Preserve consistent visual treatment across images from the same project or event.
- Store reusable lighting, posing, composition, equipment, and editing references under resources.
- Store project-specific material inside the owning project folder.
- Avoid retaining unnecessary duplicate exports or temporary files as durable knowledge.

## Useful capture fields

- Shoot or project
- Date and location
- Deliverables
- Equipment and settings
- Lighting approach
- Editing direction
- Delivery status
- Lessons learned
