# Business

Search tags: #area/business #operations #customer

## Responsibility

Maintain clear business rules, service operations, customer-facing decisions, and repeatable systems across active ventures.

## Operating standards

- Make the current objective, owner, next action, and blocker explicit.
- Separate business policy from project implementation details.
- Keep customer promises, pricing rules, availability, delivery expectations, and exceptions documented.
- Prefer workflows that reduce repeated manual work without obscuring accountability.
- Record accepted decisions in durable decision notes and keep temporary discussions out of the canonical record.
- Use project task lists for actionable work rather than embedding unchecked tasks throughout reference notes.
